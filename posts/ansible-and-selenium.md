Ansible and Selenium are both helpful for performing automation tasks — but they automate very different things.

## Ansible

Ansible has different types of pre-written code that help us a lot:

- **Galaxy** — community-shared roles you can reuse
- **Playbook** — YAML files describing the desired state of your systems
- **Ad-hoc** — one-off commands for quick tasks
